# hybridclock: How long have hybrids been forming?

Estimates the history of hybrid populations from diagnostic markers that
need no reference genome: the average age of the hybrids, how long ago
the rarer species' ancestry entered, how many times the hybrids have
backcrossed, and whether new hybridization is still happening.
Population samples are compared with simulated histories that include
drift, small breeding groups, sibling mating and separate breeding
pools, so that inbreeding is not mistaken for age. Recent hybrids can
also be classified individually.

## Details

The usual workflow is
[`diagnostic_markers()`](https://paulmaier.github.io/hybridclock/reference/diagnostic_markers.md)
and
[`hybrid_scores()`](https://paulmaier.github.io/hybridclock/reference/hybrid_scores.md),
then
[`classify_hybrids()`](https://paulmaier.github.io/hybridclock/reference/classify_hybrids.md)
for individuals, and
[`build_reference()`](https://paulmaier.github.io/hybridclock/reference/build_reference.md),
[`train_clock()`](https://paulmaier.github.io/hybridclock/reference/train_clock.md)
and
[`estimate_history()`](https://paulmaier.github.io/hybridclock/reference/estimate_history.md)
for populations.
